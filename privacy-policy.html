Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tracked ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Tracked mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: if you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored securely — we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Tracked product data: URLs, product names, prices, and price history for items you choose to track.
  • Notification data: a device push token, used solely to deliver price-change notifications to your device, if you enable them.
  • Guest mode data: if you use Tracked without an account, your tracked products are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers except when needed to fetch price data.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To run the daily automated price checks and any manual "Check now" requests you trigger.
  • To send you push notifications about price changes, if enabled.
  • To improve the reliability of price detection across supported retailers.

Third-Party Services

Tracked relies on the following third-party providers to operate:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider, which stores your account and tracked-product data.
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging — used to deliver push notifications to your device.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Retailer Websites

To check prices, Tracked automatically reads the product page URLs you add. We only read publicly available price and product information from these pages — we do not log into any retailer account on your behalf, and we are not affiliated with the retailers whose pages we read.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual tracked products, folders, or your entire account at any time from within the app. Deleting your account permanently removes your tracked products, price history, and account information from our servers. If you need help, contact us at ceciliesl@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Tracked is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ceciliesl@gmail.com.
